---
title: Quickstart
description: Create a project, add the tracker, and see your first verified page view in about five minutes.
icon: Rocket
---

<Steps>
<Step>
### Sign in

Open [app.usagemap.dev](https://app.usagemap.dev) and sign in with a **Stellar wallet**: Freighter, xBull, Albedo,
LOBSTR, Hana or any wallet that can sign a message. Hardware wallets and multisig accounts can sign a transaction
instead. There is no password to create.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create your project

Choose **Find your project** to look your project up in the public Stellar project registries and fill the form in
for you, or **Create a project** and type a name and a short name (used in links).

<Callout type="info">
  Some projects are reviewed before they count traffic. Until then the project shows **Awaiting approval**.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Add your website

In the project, choose **Add a website**. Keep the defaults (profile **standard**, visitor identifier **On**) unless
you need the [strict profile](/web#profiles). Then open **Settings → Domains** and add the domain your site runs
on.
</Step>

<Step>
### Paste the snippet

Open the website's **Snippet** tab and copy the tag. It already contains your site key and the right `integrity`
hash. Put it in the `<head>` of every page:

```html title="index.html"
<script
  src="https://e1.usagemap.dev/lib/1.2.0/standard.js"
  integrity="sha384-LQ6Yh0GTmU5/SqFMPOIB8GnEv0brlfwn9tNxj8RHKmJtQLsApBuHoZnJ2M+/CyId"
  crossorigin="anonymous"
  data-site="YOUR_SITE_KEY"
  defer
></script>
```

Using a framework? See [framework guides](/web/frameworks).
</Step>

<Step>
### See your first event

Deploy, open your site in a normal browser window, then open the website's **Installation check** tab. Within about a
minute it shows your first event and whether it was counted.

<Callout type="warn" title="Testing locally?">
  Events from `localhost` or preview URLs are only counted after you add them as a **dev origin**, and they are kept
  for 24 hours, never mixed into your real numbers. Browsers with Global Privacy Control turned on (Brave does this by
  default) send nothing at all, by design.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Verify your domain

Back in **Settings → Domains**, choose **Verify by** DNS TXT record, a file under `/.well-known/`, or an HTML meta tag.
Until your domain is verified, its page views are marked as *suspect*.
</Step>
</Steps>
