UsageMap tells you how your Stellar project is used: **visits to your website, wallet connections, calls to your API and
invocations of your contracts**, in one dashboard. It does this without cookies, without storing IP addresses, and
without ever recording a visitor's wallet address.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
**You add a small tracker** (about 3 KB) to your site, or point your API's OpenTelemetry exporter at UsageMap.
</Step>
<Step>
**UsageMap checks every event.** Bots, data-centre traffic and replayed requests are filtered out or marked as
suspect, so your numbers hold up when someone else looks at them.
</Step>
<Step>
**You see verified numbers** in the dashboard, and you can share them on a public page, as a badge or as a signed
snapshot.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Privacy by design" type="success">
  No cookies, no fingerprinting, no IP addresses, no query strings, no page titles and no wallet addresses are ever
  stored. Whether your site still needs a consent prompt depends on where you and your visitors are, so a consent
  mode is built in. [What we collect →](/privacy)
</Callout>
