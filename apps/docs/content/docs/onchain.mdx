UsageMap can count how often your contracts are used on the Stellar network: **daily invocations, successful
invocations and distinct callers**, on mainnet. It reads public ledger data; you don't install anything.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Prove you own a Stellar account

In your project, open **Verification** and link the Stellar account that deployed (or administers) your contracts, by
signing a message with it.
</Step>
<Step>
### Register your contracts

Open **Set-up wizard → Stellar contracts** (or **Register your Stellar contracts**) and add each contract ID (`C…`)
with its network, mainnet or testnet. A contract is **verified** when its deployer or admin is one of your linked
accounts.

No contracts? Choose **no footprint** so the dashboard stops asking.
</Step>
<Step>
### See the numbers

The **Overview** tile **On-chain invocations** shows the last 30 days for your verified mainnet contracts, and the
website's **Wallets** tab shows the same counts next to the wallet funnel.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Counting has to be switched on">
  On-chain counting is off until a project owner or admin turns it on. There is no switch in the app yet: send
  `PATCH /v1/tenants/{project_id}` with `{"onchain_correlation_enabled": true}` while signed in. The project's
  **Overview** shows whether it is on.
</Callout>

<Callout>
  On-chain counts are shown **next to** your web and wallet numbers, never joined with them: UsageMap does not know
  which visitor made which transaction, by design.
</Callout>

Only contract IDs can be registered today. Issuing accounts, app accounts and WASM hashes aren't supported yet.

## Register from your deploy script

A **footprint registration key** (`scfa_reg_…`) lets your deploy script or the Scaffold Stellar template add each
newly deployed contract as `claimed`, so you don't have to type contract IDs into the app. It can do nothing else.
See [Developer tools](/tools#footprint-registration-keys); example: `examples/footprint-register`.

## DeFi protocols your users touch

Once a contract is verified, the **Protocols** page of your project shows, per day, how many of the transactions that
touch your contracts also touch **Blend, Soroswap, DeFindex, Aquarius or Reflector**. The counts come from the public
ledger and are exact, but they are **lower bounds**: contracts a protocol creates at run time that UsageMap's curated
list does not include are not seen. Only the protocol name is kept, never an account or another contract. These
figures are for your team; SCF operators see them per project, never on a public page.
