## Website analytics

<Features>
  <Feature icon={<Eye />} title="Page views and visitors">
    Page views, unique visitors, visits, bounce rate and average visit duration, with a verified vs raw toggle.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<ChartColumn />} title="Breakdowns">
    Referrers, top pages, countries, devices, browsers, operating systems and your own custom events.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Route />} title="Single-page apps">
    Route changes are picked up automatically in Next.js, React, Vue, Svelte and any app using the History API.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<ListFilter />} title="Funnels, goals and journeys">
    Build a funnel from pages and events, set goals, and see the paths people take through your site.
  </Feature>
</Features>

## Stellar-specific

<Features>
  <Feature icon={<Wallet />} title="Wallet funnel">
    From "wallet dialog opened" to "transaction submitted", by wallet type (Freighter, xBull, LOBSTR, Privy, Ledger, …)
    and network, with failed connects and their reason, and the WalletConnect funnel.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<KeyRound />} title="Passkeys and smart wallets">
    Count passkey sign-ins and smart wallets created with Passkey Kit or Smart Account Kit.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Blocks />} title="On-chain usage">
    Daily invocations, successes and distinct callers of your verified Soroban contracts on mainnet, and how many of
    those transactions also touch Blend, Soroswap, DeFindex, Aquarius or Reflector.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Network />} title="Horizon and RPC surfaces">
    Latency and outcomes of the Stellar RPC and Horizon calls your backend (Node.js, Python) and your web pages make.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Landmark />} title="Payments and anchors">
    x402 and MPP payment counts, calls to SCF Integration List partners, and SEP anchor funnels from the wallet or the
    Anchor Platform side.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Smartphone />} title="Mobile apps">
    Named app events from React Native, Expo, Flutter and Telegram Mini Apps, labelled self-attested.
  </Feature>
</Features>

## API analytics

<Features>
  <Feature icon={<Activity />} title="Requests and errors">
    Requests, 4xx and 5xx errors and status codes per route, from standard OpenTelemetry.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Timer />} title="Latency">
    p50, p95 and p99 latency per route, plus an availability objective with an error budget.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Code />} title="No code changes">
    For Node.js, Python and Java, environment variables are enough. Go and Rust need a few lines; edge runtimes one
    wrapper.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<ShieldCheck />} title="Safe by default">
    Account IDs, contract IDs, secrets and e-mails in paths are replaced before storage. Query strings are always dropped.
  </Feature>
</Features>

## Trust and sharing

<Features>
  <Feature icon={<BadgeCheck />} title="Verified numbers">
    Every event is checked for bots, automation, data-centre traffic and replays. You always see how much was filtered and why.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<TriangleExclamationPoint />} title="Anomaly flags">
    Unusual spikes are flagged, and you can annotate a day ("launch on Product Hunt").
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Globe />} title="Public stats page and badge">
    Your dashboard is private. Public stats are optional and off by default: opt in to a public page for your project,
    and embed a live badge in your README or site.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Signature />} title="Signed snapshots">
    Freeze a period's numbers into a signed snapshot that anyone can verify, for example for a grant report.
  </Feature>
</Features>

## Privacy

<Features>
  <Feature icon={<Cookie />} title="No cookies">
    Nothing is stored in the visitor's browser except an opt-out flag, if they choose to opt out.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<EyeOff />} title="Nothing personal">
    No IP addresses, raw user agents, query strings, page titles or wallet addresses are ever stored.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<Hand />} title="Respects the visitor">
    Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are honoured. An optional consent mode waits for your banner.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<MapPin />} title="Hosted in the EU">
    Data is processed and stored in the EU, with backups in the same region.
  </Feature>
</Features>

<Features>

    [For AI agents →](/ai-agents)
  </Feature>
</Features>

## Service levels

The service-level targets are **99.99 % ingest availability** and **99.90 % dashboard availability** per calendar
month, measured and published every month, with a post-mortem for every miss. The current single-host deployment has
its own objective of **99.5 %** until the high-availability profile runs. Live status:
[status.usagemap.dev](https://status.usagemap.dev).

<Callout title="Not available yet">
  E-mail alerts, webhooks and a public directory of projects are not part of UsageMap today.
</Callout>
