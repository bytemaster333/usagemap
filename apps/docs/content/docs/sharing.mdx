**Who sees what.** Your project's dashboard is private to your team. SCF operators have a global view
across all projects, so they can review the programme. Public stats are optional and **off by default**: nothing about
your project is public until you turn them on. All sharing is set up under the project's **Sharing** and
**Traction snapshots** tabs.

## Share keys

To share your metrics document without making it public, create a **share key** (`scfa_pub_…`) under **Sharing** and
send it in a header:

```sh
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $USAGEMAP_SHARE_KEY" \
  https://app.usagemap.dev/public/v1/projects/your-short-name/metrics.json
```

A share key only reads that one document. It can't see dashboards, change settings or send data. Keys in a URL are
refused, so they don't end up in logs.

## Anonymous reports

Under a website's **Settings**, **Anonymous reports** hides rows with fewer than 10 visitors and rounds the rest to
the nearest 10, so that small numbers can't single anyone out.
